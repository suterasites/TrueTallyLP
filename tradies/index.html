The Problem

If your bookkeeper has never costed a job, your numbers are guesswork.

BAS deadlines, GST on materials and labour, subbies and PAYG, super, TPAR, and no real idea which jobs actually made money. Most bookkeepers aren't set up for any of it. We are.

BAS creeps up while you're on site

Quarterly BAS, GST split across materials and labour, and cash flow that lags the work you've already done. Miss the timing and you're paying the ATO money you've already spent on the next job.

Subbies, PAYG and super are a minefield

Is that worker a subbie or an employee? PAYG withholding, super guarantee, and the Taxable Payments Annual Report (TPAR) all hang off getting it right. Get it wrong and the ATO comes knocking.

You don't know which jobs actually made money

Materials, labour, fuel, tools and equipment all eat your margin. Without job-costing, the busy jobs feel profitable and the quiet ones hide the losses. Guesswork is expensive.
